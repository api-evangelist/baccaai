# Bacca.ai

> Bacca.ai is an AI-driven Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) platform that deploys an autonomous agent acting as a Tier-1 on-call engineer. It ingests operational context from sources such as Slack, Jira, logs, traces, and metrics to build a proprietary Knowledge Graph of an organization's systems, then contextualizes alerts, identifies root causes, automates incident war rooms and post-mortems, and reports on system hotspots — cutting Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR) by roughly 40-60%. Founded in 2023, San Francisco. Backed by Homebrew.
